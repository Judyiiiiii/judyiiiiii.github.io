## Initial illustration prompt

Create a highly detailed fine graphite pencil drawing for an interactive personal engineering portfolio website. Wide landscape composition, 16:9. A whimsical hand-drawn electronics workshop seen in three-quarter frontal perspective, like a walkable room drawn by a talented architect in an engineering sketchbook. The room occupies the right 75% of the canvas; the left 25% is almost empty warm off-white crumpled sketch paper for HTML text overlay. A long simple wooden workbench in the room, a small retro oscilloscope with a clear waveform in its screen at the far right, an open small laptop on the middle-left of the desk with only a simple envelope outline drawn on the screen, a small breadboard and sensors at the front-left of the desk. Several deliberately oversized electronic components (a bent axial resistor, a cylindrical capacitor, a square integrated circuit with pins) appear as carefully drawn sculptural objects around the bench; thin connecting wires curl across the table. A pencil and open schematic notebook near front edge. Faint architectural perspective walls, window and drafting marks make it feel like an explorable real space. All objects outlined in slightly imperfect doubled pencil lines, dense crosshatching on sides, delicate graphite grain, very light smudges, authentic hand-drawn finish, generous negative space, mostly monochrome warm ivory and charcoal; only tiny muted terracotta dots on two LEDs. Loose expressive but precise drawing; airy and bright. Paper is warm nearly-white #f5f3ed, not yellow, with subtle crumples. No text, no lettering, no UI, no logos, no numbers, no cards, no rounded graphic panels, no photographs, no 3D-rendered plastic appearance. This is an original decorative illustration, not a depiction of the student's actual hardware.
